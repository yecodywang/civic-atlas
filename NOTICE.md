# Notice: independent, unofficial project

civic-atlas is an **independent, volunteer-run project**. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by,
or operated by** the City of Raleigh, Wake County, Durham County, the State of North Carolina, or
any other government or organization it describes.

- **Not an official source.** Every catalog entry links to the official page it was compiled from.
  Services, phone numbers, hours and rules change. Always confirm with the official source before
  you rely on something, and especially before you pay a bill, miss a deadline or skip a step.
- **Emergencies:** if anyone is in danger, call **911**. This project is not an emergency service.
- **No submissions on your behalf.** The civic-atlas software never contacts government systems,
  never files requests and never makes payments. You always complete each step yourself, on the
  official website, app or phone line.
- **No logos or seals.** We do not use government logos, seals or trademarks. Names of governments,
  departments and programs appear only to describe them accurately.
- **Wording.** How-to steps are written in our own words. We do not copy official page text or
  images. Facts such as phone numbers and web addresses are stated with a link to their source.
- **Corrections.** If something here is wrong, please tell us. See [CORRECTIONS.md](CORRECTIONS.md).
  If you are government staff, we especially welcome corrections from you.
