# civic-atlas

**An open, community-maintained guide to local government services, starting with Raleigh, North Carolina.**

> **Independent and unofficial.** civic-atlas is a volunteer project. It is not affiliated with or
> endorsed by the City of Raleigh, Wake County, the State of North Carolina, or any other
> government. Always confirm details on the official site linked in each entry.
>
> **Not for emergencies.** civic-atlas covers non-emergency services only. In an emergency,
> contact emergency services directly.

## Printable guide for Raleigh

- [Task menu: pick three tasks to get done](cards/us-nc-place-raleigh/README.md)
- [Who do I contact? (City, County or State)](cards/us-nc-place-raleigh/who-to-contact.md)
- [Key contacts to save on your phone](cards/us-nc-place-raleigh/key-contacts.md)

Every card says where its facts came from and when they were last checked. All entries are
currently **drafts** that a volunteer hasn't verified yet.

## Use it with an AI assistant

civic-atlas includes a small, offline MCP server so Claude and other assistants can answer
questions from the catalog. See the
[project README on GitHub](https://github.com/yecodywang/civic-atlas#quick-start).
